# Makkah Laundry Center — Vite React

Professional laundry care across Makkah, with simple WhatsApp booking, careful
garment handling, and pickup from your hotel or home.

## Target customers

- Umrah/Hajj pilgrims
- Hotel guests
- Makkah residents & families
- Hospitality businesses

## Services

Wash-and-fold, dry cleaning, ironing, stain treatment, perfume-free Ihram care,
thobes, abayas, blankets, curtains, and express service.

## Positioning

> Professional laundry care across Makkah, with simple WhatsApp booking, careful
> garment handling, and pickup from your hotel or home.

Emphasis on separate-order washing and perfume-free Ihram care (key
differentiators for pilgrims). Avoid exaggerated claims; compete on credibility.

## WhatsApp booking flow

1. Select a service
2. Enter name, hotel/home location, room number, pickup time
3. Send prepared order to WhatsApp
4. Staff confirm timing and estimated price
5. Driver collects, counts, labels garments
6. Customer receives progress updates
7. Clean laundry delivered; payment collected

## Project structure

- **`public/assets/`** — static images, fonts, stylesheets, vendor scripts
- **`src/pages/`** — page modules (28 pages currently)
- **`src/routes/`** — route definitions (`AppRouter.jsx`, `routes.jsx`)
- **`src/components/`** — shared UI components
- **`src/data/`** — editable content (services, testimonials)
- **`src/hooks/`** — custom React hooks
- **`mail.php`** — PHP contact form handler
